What these reports show

Both formats show the same genuine Chromium + API smoke execution, recorded on 17 October 2026, 15:30 UTC. They are a saved snapshot, not a live availability check. Open individual test cases to see their steps and timings.

The smoke suite covers registration, login, access restrictions, catalog/search, cart, profile data and real UI/API product comparisons. Sensitive generated test values and machine identifiers were redacted before publication; results and timings were preserved.

Full regression: 58 of 61 passed. Three checks remain active and failing against the hosted application: fresh empty-cart rendering, credit-card required-field feedback and invoice PDF generation.
